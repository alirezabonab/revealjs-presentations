Prices are estimates. The calculation uses vendor list prices per million tokens on 2 October 2026. One attempt uses 400k input tokens and 60k output tokens across its turns. Low effort uses the list price × 0.58. Medium uses × 0.78. Anthropic measured these cost ratios on tasks where extra effort made little difference. The map applies them to every vendor as an estimate. Prices exclude extra attempts by other agents. No ratio is published for extra high or maximum effort. These levels show the high-effort price with a + sign.

Fit is an estimate. The task examples were measured on Opus 5, Sonnet 5, and Fable 5. The map judges each model by its capability group. We assigned these groups from vendor descriptions. We did not measure the differences between vendors. Try your task on two models before you rely on the map.

Effort settings differ. Extra high means xhigh. Maximum means max. Haiku 4.5 and Grok 4.3 have no effort setting. Grok 4.7 stops at xhigh. GPT-6 Luna and GPT-5.6 Terra also offer none. Opus 5.5 defaults to medium. Sonnet 5.5’s effort levels have changed. Test these models at several effort levels. Composer 2.5’s five levels come from Cursor’s release coverage, not its documentation.

Multiple agents share the work. In Claude Code, ultracode uses multiple agents. In Codex, GPT-6.1 Sol offers ultra, an effort level above max. On Grok 4.20 multi-agent, the effort setting controls the number of agents. The map allows multiple agents only for models in the Workhorse and Frontier groups.

Some rates differ. Grok doubles its rate for requests above 200k tokens. The map uses the base rate. Composer 2.5 Fast costs $3 for input and $15 for output per million tokens. Opus 5.5 fast mode costs $8 for input and $40 for output per million tokens. Cursor also offers models from other vendors. Teams and Enterprise add $0.25 per million tokens. GPT-5.6 Terra is an older generation that is still sold.

Get approval before use. Fable 5.1 requires the vendor to retain data for 30 days. The CTO and Compliance must approve its use first. If you use a new vendor through its API, DORA treats this as an information and communication technology (ICT) third-party arrangement.
